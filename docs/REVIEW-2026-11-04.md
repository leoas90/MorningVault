# MorningVault — product + code review, 2026-11-04

Scope: whole app at `37af19c` (Brief, Markets, Meetings/`MeetingNotesKit`, Settings, widget, Siri intents, onboarding, privacy manifest, docs). Ranked by impact for a daily user. Items marked **[fixed]** were applied in this pass; everything else is a recommendation.

## 1. Inconsistencies (ranked)

1. **Two accent systems.** Brief/Markets/Settings use the theme-aware `Color.warmPrimaryAccent` (tan `D4A373` warm / blue-gray cool / sage calm / gray dark). The Meetings package and all onboarding screens use `Color.accentColor`, which is the asset-catalog **system blue** (`AccentColor.colorset` = 0/0.478/1). Result: "You" speaker labels, action-item checkmarks, enrollment confirmation, onboarding chips/buttons are iOS blue while the rest of the app is warm. `.tint()` on the TabView does not change `Color.accentColor`. Fix: set the asset AccentColor to the warm tan, or have the package read `.tint`/a passed-in accent.
2. **Meetings ignores Appearance/Theme.** Brief paints `Color.warmBackground`/`warmSurface` cards (radius `AppRadius.card` = 20); Meetings is a plain `List` with `.thinMaterial` cards at radius 14 and `Color.primary.opacity(...)` fills; Markets is a plain `List` too. The Theme picker (System/Light/Dark) does propagate (via `preferredColorScheme`), the Appearance picker (Warm/Cool/Calm) does not reach Meetings or Markets at all.
3. **Nav title mode.** Brief, Markets, Settings: inline. Meetings was large. **[fixed → inline]**
4. **Brand spelling.** "Morning Vault" (Brief nav title, notification title, BuildView) vs "MorningVault" (everywhere else, share text, Siri). **[fixed → MorningVault]**
5. **Leftover "Vault Notes" / `VaultNotes` from the package's previous life.** User-facing: "Turn it on in Settings › Vault Notes › Calendar/Reminders" (wrong — the Settings entry is MorningVault) **[fixed]**; env key `vaultNotesSampleMode` **[fixed → `meetingSampleMode`]**; on-disk root `Application Support/VaultNotes` — **left as is** (holds TestFlight users' meetings; rename only with a one-time move migration).
6. **British spelling in user-visible copy**: "recognised" (transcript sheet), "summarise" (summarizer error), "enrol again" (voice profile delete alert), "summarised" (Settings PCC footer). **[fixed → US]**. Internal identifiers/comments (`labelledTranscript`, "finalised", "analyser") left alone.
7. **Settings › Permissions was incomplete**: listed HealthKit, Calendar, Location, Notifications only; Meetings needs Microphone, Speech Recognition and Reminders. **[fixed: three rows added, status + request/open Settings]**. Still inaccurate: the "Alarm" row and `lastError` are shown twice (Alarm section *and* Permissions section).
8. **Local-Only Mode disables the Health and Calendar toggles** even though both are on-device sources. Only Weather/Headlines/Markets go online. Confusing for exactly the privacy-minded user the mode targets.
9. **Tab icons**: Brief `sun.max`→`sun.max.fill`, Settings `gearshape`→`.fill`, Markets same glyph both states, Meetings `waveform`→`waveform.circle.fill` (shape jump). **[fixed: Meetings uses `waveform` for both, like Markets]**. All 62 SF Symbol names in the app exist in the macOS 27 / iOS 26 catalog (checked against `CoreGlyphs name_availability.plist`).
10. **Copy case**: Settings is Title Case ("Schedule / Update Alarm", "Cancel All Alarms", "Send Test Notification", "Local-Only Mode", "Voice Briefing"), Meetings is sentence case ("Enhance notes", "Share notes", "New meeting", "Your voice"), Brief mixes ("Since yesterday", "AI Summary", "Mark all read", "Save for Later"). Meetings' sentence case matches Apple HIG; Settings should move that way. All-caps micro-headers ("AGENDA", "ATTENDEES", "TALKING POINTS", "YOUR PAST POSITIONS") appear only on the Meeting Prep card.
11. **Developer text reaching users**: Settings shows `Error: <localizedDescription>` / `Last error: …` from AlarmService; "Send Test Notification", "Refresh Status", "N active notification triggers", "✓ Scheduled" read like a debug panel. Brief error banners ("Health access denied") and Meetings banners ("Could not start transcription: <localizedDescription>") leak framework strings.
12. **Theme change resets navigation.** `ContentView` has `.id(themeRaw + appearanceRaw)` on the TabView (and `MorningVaultApp` `.id(themeRaw)` on the root): changing Theme/Appearance in Settings rebuilds the whole tab view and drops you back to the Brief tab, losing any Meetings navigation.
13. **Onboarding**: three steps (name, news sources, symbols) and never mentions Meetings, the widget or Siri. The name step says "Tap a suggestion or enter your name to continue" but nothing advances the page — only a swipe does (index dots hidden, no Next button). The sources step says "You can change these later", but **there is no Sources screen reachable anywhere** (see dead code: `SourcesView`).
14. **Brief empty state** says "Set Your Alarm / Configure your briefing time in Settings" when sections are empty — the alarm has nothing to do with loading data; the real causes are permissions/offline.
15. **Markets shows fake data**: when no live price exists a seeded random "demo" sparkline is drawn (tiny 8-pt "demo" caption). In a trust-first app, invented price curves are a brand risk. Also "Track up to 10 symbols" is stated but no cap is enforced; `MarketsViewModel.cryptoSymbols` was unused **[removed]**; comment said "from Polygon.io" **[fixed]**.
16. **Network badge has an `.email` state ("EMAIL", orange)** that can never occur — there is no email feature.
17. **`warmNegative == appAccentColor`**: a negative/down value is drawn in the accent color (tan), and in the Dark theme the accent is muted gray, so "down" and "neutral" are indistinguishable.
18. **Widget parity**: fine on content (summary, next event, headline, weather line) and honors units via App Group. Gaps: no `widgetURL`/deep link target, no Meetings info (e.g. "up next" with Record), and `.systemSmall` is handled by the `default:` branch.
19. **Siri parity**: two intents (summary, headlines). No intent for Meetings (start recording, last meeting's action items) or markets.
20. **Dynamic Type**: fixed sizes in `TabIcon` (`.system(size: 20)`), "demo" caption (`size: 8`), onboarding icon (`size: 60`); otherwise text styles are used. Accessibility labels are present on toolbar buttons and cards. Dark mode: handled via UIColor trait closures; Meetings relies on system colors, so it is fine.
21. **Info.plist**: `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` claims the app "writes your daily health summary to Apple Health" — it never writes (`requestAuthorization(toShare: [])`). Never shown to users, but false if App Review reads it; drop it. `com.apple.developer.ios.user-data-access` was a made-up key **[removed]**. `NSContactsUsageDescription` described a permission the app no longer uses **[removed with ContactsService]**.
22. **Docs**: `SPEC.md` described wttr.in, CoinGecko/yfinance, IMAP email, OpenClaw, AlarmKit — none shipped **[status banner added]**. `README.md` said iOS 26.1, Xcode 16.4, GitHub Actions CI, Polygon proxy **[rewritten]**. `CHANGELOG.txt` is a single stray line ("v7 Mon May 11…"); either maintain it or delete it.

## 3. Privacy

1. **Meeting transcripts are duplicated into the App Group container and never deleted.** `MeetingPersistence.writeToAppGroup` writes `<title>.md` (notes + full transcript + action items) to `group.com.morningvault.app/Meetings/` on every save/enhance; nothing in the app or widget reads it, and `HomeScreen.delete` removes the SwiftData rows and audio but not the markdown. Renaming a meeting produces a second file. Remove the writer or delete the file on meeting delete/rename.
2. **Privacy manifest missed `DiskSpace`.** FluidAudio's `LFM3AudioConfig.freeDiskBytes()` (`volumeAvailableCapacity`) is compiled into the binary even with only the `Diarizer` trait (verified `LFM3AudioConfig.o` in the archive). **[fixed: `NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypeDiskSpace` / `E174.1` added]**. `attributesOfItem` (FileTimestamp, `C617.1`) and `UserDefaults` (`CA92.1`) were already declared. FluidAudio ships no `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` of its own; it is not on Apple's required-SDK list, so the app manifest covers it.
3. **FluidAudio download code is linked** (`ModelHub`, `HFClient`, `FileDownloader`, `URLSession`) but `SpeakerDiarizer` only calls `DiarizerManager` with the bundled models; `ModelHub` is never invoked. Keep the comment in `Package.swift` and add a test/grep guard so a future upgrade cannot start downloading from Hugging Face.
4. **Logging**: no `print`/`os_log`/`Logger` in app or package code (only commented-out DEBUG prints). FluidAudio uses `os.Logger` for timings, not audio or text. No analytics/crash SDKs. Only outbound hosts: `api.morningvault.app`, WeatherKit, and the user's RSS feeds (`NewsSource`), consistent with "Data Not Collected".
5. **Contacts**: the app enumerated the user's "Me" contact at every launch and discarded the result **[removed]**.
6. **Health**: `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` claims a write that never happens (see Inconsistencies 21). Keep `toShare: []`; drop the key.
7. **Brief share sheet** exports health lines ("Sleep 6h 10m, HRV …") together with weather/calendar — by design, but a one-line "includes health data" hint before sharing would match the brand.
8. **App Group UserDefaults** holds the AI summary, first event title and headlines for the widget/Siri. Widget text is `privacySensitive()` (redacted on Lock Screen), Siri dialog is not — acceptable, but the summary can contain the event title and health phrasing.

## 4. Product suggestions (ranked by daily value)

1. **Wire `SourcesView` into Settings** — the onboarding promise "you can change these later" is currently false, and it is the only way to fix a bad feed choice.
2. **Meetings on the Brief**: show today's "Up next" meeting on the Brief with a Record button (the package already ships `MeetingRecordButton`), and show yesterday's open action items (`MeetingDigest` already feeds "Since yesterday") as tappable rows.
3. **Add Meetings Siri intents**: "Start a meeting in MorningVault" and "What are my action items from yesterday" — the data is on-device and these are the two things a Territory Sales Manager does in the car.
4. **One accent, one card style** across tabs (see Inconsistencies 1–2); Markets and Meetings should sit on `warmBackground` with `AppRadius.card` cards so the app reads as one product.
5. **Remove demo sparklines** in Markets; show "—" until a quote arrives, and fetch the batch in one proxy call instead of one request per symbol.
6. **Turn the Alarm section into one row**: time picker + "Weekdays" toggle, auto-apply on change; move the test notification and status details out of the main Settings screen.
7. **Keep tab/navigation state on theme change** (drop the `.id(themeRaw + appearanceRaw)` rebuild, pass colors through the environment instead).
8. **Onboarding step for Meetings** with permission priming (mic + speech) and a "Your voice" enrollment prompt, plus a real Next button on each page.
9. **Widget tap targets**: `widgetURL` to the Brief, and a medium-widget variant showing the next meeting with a "Record" Link.
10. **Make "Save for Later" mean something**: a "Saved" section at the bottom of Headlines (or delete the action).
